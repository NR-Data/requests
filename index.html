NR-Data

Community-made army list data for tabletop games. The files are made by their contributors, not by NewRecruit or by any game publisher, and nobody here is affiliated with or endorsed by those publishers.

Before adding anything, read the content rules.

No copyrighted content

Data files may hold facts: unit and option names, points, stat values, keywords, army building limits, page references. Do not upload:

  • rules, ability, spell or card text copied from books, cards, apps or websites, even retyped or lightly reworded
  • scans, photos or screenshots of books or cards
  • artwork, logos or faction symbols
  • any data for Games Workshop games (Warhammer 40,000, Age of Sigmar, Horus Heresy, The Old World, Kill Team, Necromunda, Blood Bowl, Middle-earth and others)

Content reported by a rights holder is removed. To report something, use a removal request.

How it works

  1. Each button opens a filled-in request on GitHub. Click Submit new issue there; a bot handles it within a minute or two and replies on the issue.
  2. Creating a repository makes you its head maintainer. Accounts younger than 30 days need an admin's approval.
  3. Joining a repository is immediate when it has no head maintainer, or when its head maintainer has been inactive for 90 days. Otherwise they are asked, and the request is approved automatically after 14 days without an answer.
  4. Anyone can also open a pull request on any repository without asking for access.

All requests are public.
